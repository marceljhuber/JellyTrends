JellyTrends

Requires the File Transformation plugin. Covers Jellyfin Web, the Android and iOS apps, and Jellyfin Media Player on Windows, macOS and Linux. Restart Jellyfin after turning this on.

Trending source

Automatic prefers TMDB, then Trakt, then falls back to Cinemeta. Cinemeta needs no credentials but ranks by general popularity rather than by what is trending this week.
Free from themoviedb.org under Settings → API. Unlocks the real Trending this week charts.
Free from trakt.tv/oauth/applications. Ranks by how many people are watching right now.
Used by TMDB. Other sources publish a single chart.

Display

How many titles each row shows. 10 gives the classic Top 10 look; the row headings follow this number automatically.
On: a title sitting at #37 online keeps the #37 badge even when it is the third one you own. Off: badges are renumbered 1..N.

Matching

How far down the online chart to look for titles you own. Raise this if a row comes up short on a small library.
Only fall back to title matching when the release year also matches. Provider ID matches (IMDb, TMDB, TVDB) are always used first and ignore this setting.
